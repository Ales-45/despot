You have three d6, allocate in secret a max of 10 points as defender or 11 as attacker

Resolution is made from left to right from the attacker perspective, mirrowing dices and winning the highest value or in the case of a tie the defender wins, two out of three sides are required to win the bout

The game is a best out of 3 bouts
